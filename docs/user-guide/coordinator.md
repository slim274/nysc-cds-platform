# CDS Coordinator Guide

The Coordinator workspace is available at `/coordinator`. A Coordinator can operate only the CDS group(s) assigned by an LGI. Access to each group is checked by the API, not only by the menu.

## Group overview

The dashboard summarizes your assigned groups, active member counts, President assignments and upcoming meetings. Open a group to review its active roster and course details. Use the dashboard quick links to reach the supporting group modules.

## Designate or replace a CDS President

Choose an active Corps Member from the roster of the relevant assigned group, then use **Designate President**. The President is an existing Corps Member account; the platform does not create a duplicate person record.

To replace a President, select another eligible active group member. The designation history records current and previous appointments. The President role remains group-scoped and does not allow the President to change group membership or appoint another President.

## WhatsApp group settings

For each assigned group, the Coordinator can save or remove the WhatsApp invitation link and designate a group assistant from the existing group members. Keep the invite link current. An eligible Corps Member sees **Join WhatsApp Group** in their portal only when the assigned group has a link configured.

## Meetings and attendance

Open **Attendance** to schedule a meeting for one of your assigned groups. Select the meeting to review its attendance workflow.

1. The CDS President opens the scheduled meeting and records each active member as Present, Absent, Late or Excused.
2. The President submits the attendance register to the Coordinator.
3. The Coordinator reviews the submitted register.
4. Approve an accurate register or return it for correction. A returned register can be edited and submitted again by the President.
5. Once approved, the register is locked and approved attendance becomes visible in the Corps Member portal.

Do not approve a register until its entries have been checked against the meeting and group roster.

## Published document checklist

Open **Documents** to see the document requirements published by the LGI and the submission status for members in your assigned group(s). This view is for checklist visibility and follow-up. It does not let a Coordinator create or change the LGI's document requirements.

Document approval and resubmission decisions are handled by authorized LGI reviewers or CLO officers through their document-review workspace.

## Course Mapping

Open **Course Mapping** to view and configure the course mappings for your assigned groups. Map only courses that are relevant to the group's intended membership. These mappings influence the platform's course-based eligibility for group placement. The shared canonical course list itself is managed by the LGI through the Course Catalogue.

## Announcements

Use the Coordinator announcements workspace to communicate with assigned group members. President-submitted announcements are routed to the Coordinator for review; approve or decline them before they are published to members. Check the title, message, priority and expiry information before approving.

## Handover inbox

When an LGI transfers a group to you, open **Handover Inbox**. Review the recorded notes, pending activities, upcoming events and outstanding tasks. Select **Review complete — accept handover** after you have reviewed the information. The acceptance note and timestamp are retained.

An LGI handover changes the active Coordinator assignment when the transfer is made. Your acceptance records that you have reviewed and acknowledged the handover; it is not an NYSC clearance or an official external approval.

For invitation, account activation, group assignment, disable/reactivate and transfer rules, see [Coordinator Management](../coordinator-management.md).

## Monthly clearance

Use the Coordinator **Monthly Clearance** page to review the monthly register and record the available status decisions for members within your scope. Review the member's history before changing an existing decision. The LGI has its own oversight and monthly clearance workspace.

## Access and support

If a group is missing, ask the LGI administrator to check your active assignment. Do not try to use an ID or link belonging to a group outside your assignment.
