# Corps Liaison Officer (CLO) Guide

The CLO document workspace is available at `/clo/documents`. CLO accounts are created and managed by LGI administrators.

## Access and scope

The LGI chooses one or more LGA management areas when creating or updating a CLO account. That selection determines which LGI administrators can manage the CLO account. The CLO's document workspace itself is platform-wide: it can handle document submissions for Corps Members across every LGA.

If the account is disabled or its access is paused, contact an LGI administrator responsible for the account's management scope.

## Review submissions

The workspace provides:
- Active document requirements published by LGIs.
- Corps Members with submissions across every LGA.
- Submission counts and statuses, grouped so the reviewer can inspect a member's documents together.

Open a submission using **View document** and inspect the file before deciding. Where a human decision is pending, choose:
- **Mark verified** to accept the document.
- **Request resubmission** to return it with a clear correction instruction.

A review note of at least 10 characters is required. The platform records the decision, reviewer and time. Do not approve a file based only on its filename or an AI recommendation.

## CLO permission boundaries

A CLO can review document submissions and view active requirements. A CLO cannot create, edit, enable, disable or remove document requirements. Those settings remain under LGI administration.

The LGA scope displayed in the account form controls which LGI administrators can manage the CLO account; it does not restrict the CLO document queue to those LGAs.

## Account administration

For account creation, activation, management scope and access pause/restore, contact the LGI administrator. Do not share activation tokens or passwords in group chats.
