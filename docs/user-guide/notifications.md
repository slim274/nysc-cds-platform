# Notifications

Authenticated users can access notifications from the bell icon in the application header. The notification centre is available at `/notifications`.

## Read notifications

Open the notification centre to view the items available to your account. Select an item to open the associated page or context; opening an item marks it as read where supported.

Use **Mark all as read** to clear the unread state for all notifications in your account. The notification list is personal to the signed-in user.

If a notification link leads to a page you cannot access, the relevant role assignment may have changed or the linked item may be outside your current scope. Contact the appropriate LGI or Coordinator.

Do not treat a browser notification as evidence that a document, attendance register or clearance decision has been approved. Check the relevant record in the platform.
